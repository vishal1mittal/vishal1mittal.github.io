About Cambridge

Empowering students with industry-relevant skills since 2010. We are committed to bridging the gap between education and employment.

Our Story

A Decade of Excellence in Tech Education

Cambridge Computer Education started with a simple mission: to provide high-quality, affordable computer education to the youth of Rohini, Delhi. Over the years, we have evolved from a small coaching center to a premier institute offering advanced courses in Data Science, Web Development, and Graphic Design.

We take pride in our "Practical First" approach, ensuring that every student who walks out of our doors is ready to face the real-world challenges of the IT industry.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted hub for technical education in North Delhi, creating a community of skilled professionals who drive innovation and growth.

Our Mission

To provide accessible, high-quality technical training that adapts to the changing industry landscape, fostering career growth for every student regardless of their background.
